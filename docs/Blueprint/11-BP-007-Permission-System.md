# BP-007 — Permission System Principle

## Blueprint ID

BP-007

---

## Title

Permission System Principle

---

## Purpose

The Permission System Principle defines how RIN safely interacts with the digital and physical environments of the Primary Owner.

Every capability that can affect data, devices, communications, or personal information must operate within a transparent and permission-controlled framework.

Permission is the foundation of trust.

---

# Vision

Powerful capabilities require responsible control.

RIN shall never assume permission.

Instead, permissions shall be explicitly granted, clearly understood, and fully controllable by the Primary Owner.

The objective is to maximize trust without unnecessarily reducing usability.

---

# Core Principles

## Owner Authority

The Primary Owner has complete authority over every permission granted to RIN.

Permissions may be granted, reviewed, modified, or revoked at any time.

---

## Least Privilege

Every capability should receive only the minimum permissions required to perform its intended function.

No unnecessary access shall be requested or retained.

---

## Transparency

Whenever permissions are requested or used, RIN should clearly explain:

- Why the permission is required.
- What capability depends on it.
- What information or resource will be accessed.

Permission usage should never become hidden behavior.

---

## Explicit Consent

Sensitive actions require explicit approval from the Primary Owner unless previously approved according to the defined permission policies.

---

## Revocability

Every permission granted to RIN shall remain reversible.

The Primary Owner should be able to withdraw permissions without compromising the overall stability of the system.

---

## Security Integration

Permissions shall operate together with authentication, identity management, encryption, and security monitoring to provide layered protection.

---

## Continuous Trust

Permission management is not a one-time event.

It is an ongoing relationship that preserves confidence between the Primary Owner and RIN.

---

# Expected Outcome

The Permission System enables RIN to perform meaningful actions while preserving security, privacy, transparency, and complete owner control.

Every future capability shall respect the permission architecture established by this blueprint.

---

# Official Blueprint Statement

> "Every capability within RIN shall earn trust through transparent, permission-based operation that always preserves the authority of the Primary Owner."
