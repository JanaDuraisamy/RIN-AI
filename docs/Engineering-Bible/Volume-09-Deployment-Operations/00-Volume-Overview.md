Deployment & Operations

# Purpose

This volume defines how the RIN ecosystem is built, deployed, operated, monitored, maintained, and continuously improved throughout its operational lifetime.

Deployment is not the end of engineering.

Deployment marks the beginning of responsible operations.

---

# Objectives

This volume establishes:

- Operational Philosophy
- Build Pipeline
- Deployment Standards
- Runtime Operations
- Monitoring
- Logging
- Updates
- Business Continuity

---

# Scope

This volume applies to:

- Local Development
- Production Deployment
- Runtime Infrastructure
- Monitoring Systems
- Operational Engineering
- Release Operations

---

# Official Statement

Every deployment within the RIN ecosystem shall remain reliable, repeatable, observable, and maintainable through disciplined operational engineering.
