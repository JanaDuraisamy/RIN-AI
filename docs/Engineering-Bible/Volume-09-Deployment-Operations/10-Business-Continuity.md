# Business Continuity

## Introduction

Business Continuity defines the engineering principles, operational strategies, and recovery processes that enable the RIN ecosystem to continue operating during unexpected disruptions.

Operational continuity extends beyond backup and recovery.

Its objective is to maintain essential capabilities, minimize service interruption, and preserve user trust under adverse conditions.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of Business Continuity is to ensure that critical RIN capabilities remain available or recover within acceptable engineering objectives following operational disruptions.

Continuity planning strengthens long-term resilience.

---

# Responsibilities

The Business Continuity System is responsible for:

- Continuity Planning
- Critical Service Identification
- Operational Resilience
- Disaster Preparedness
- Recovery Coordination
- Service Restoration
- Continuity Validation
- Continuous Improvement

# Continuity Principles

## Preparation

Continuity shall be planned before operational disruption occurs.

---

## Resilience

Critical services should tolerate failures whenever practical.

---

## Recovery

Recovery procedures shall remain documented, tested, and repeatable.

---

## Verification

Operational recovery shall be verified before declaring continuity restored.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Every disruption shall improve future continuity planning.

# Best Practices

- Identify critical services.
- Test continuity procedures regularly.
- Maintain updated recovery documentation.
- Review operational risks.
- Improve resilience continuously.

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Assuming failures will never occur.
- Untested recovery procedures.
- Missing operational documentation.
- Ignoring continuity reviews.
- Declaring recovery without verification.

---

# Engineering Checklist

Before approving Business Continuity:

- Critical services identified.
- Continuity plans documented.
- Recovery tested.
- Monitoring operational.
- Lessons learned incorporated.
